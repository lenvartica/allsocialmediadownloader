Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

What we process

When you paste a link, it is sent to our server only to look up the video and prepare the download. We do not store the links, the videos or any personal account details.

Analytics & advertising pixels

If enabled by the site owner, we may use Meta Pixel, Google Analytics, TikTok Pixel or Microsoft Clarity to measure visits and improve the service. These load only when configured and are skipped when your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.

Cookies

The site itself sets no cookies. Third-party analytics tools, if enabled, may set their own.

Your content

Only download content you own or have permission to save. You are responsible for respecting copyright and each platform's terms.
